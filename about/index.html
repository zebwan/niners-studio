Our story

From one bay to
a detailing brand

The first bay

9NERS opens its doors in Taman Desa Tebrau — a small crew, a tool wall, and the now-famous pink checkered floor. Registered as NINERS DETAILING STUDIO (KT0551869-V).

More than cars

Bike spa, helmet coating and babygear refresh join the menu — because everything you ride and everything your kids sit in deserves the same standard.

Teaching the craft

The 9NERS Detailing Academy launches at our Eco Cascadia studio — personal, hands-on classes taking beginners from kelas asas cucian to machine polishing.

Our own formulas

Together with OSREN, we launch 9ice — Pinky Gum, Oceanic Rush and Arctic White — the same shampoos we use in the studio, now on Shopee, Lazada and TikTok Shop.
