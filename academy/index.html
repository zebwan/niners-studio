What you'll learn

Paint correction,
start to finish

Every class runs on real cars with real studio tools — the same process we use on customer vehicles every day.

1

Wash

Safe wash technique, chemistry, and the mistakes that put swirls into paint.

2

Decontamination

Iron fallout, tar and clay bar work — getting the paint truly clean first.

3

Masking

Protecting trim, rubber and edges before the machine touches the car.

4

Polishing

Compound, polish and finish — pad and liquid selection, machine control, reading the paint.

5

Protection

Sealing the work in, plus aftercare advice you can pass on to your own customers.
