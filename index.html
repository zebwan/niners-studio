What we do

Pick your treatment

Every package is done in-studio by our own detailers. No shortcuts, no outsourcing.

  • Polish & Paint Correction From 1-step gloss to 3-step advance correction for heavy swirls and acid rain etching. from RM 330
  • 9NERS Signature Wash Our baseline for every car: hand-brushed detailing wash, clay bar, fine gap & edge cleaning, ceramic spray seal and reblack trim. from RM 88
  • Ceramic Coating 8H and 9H crystallised coatings. Glossier than new paint, protected for one or two years, with free mirror treatments included. from RM 850
  • Paint Protection Film Clear armour against stone chips, scratches and UV. Full front in 2–3 days, or panel by panel: mirrors, headlamps, even door handles. from RM 120
  • Interior, Leather & Babygear Leather refresh, fabric extraction cleans, seat-out interior resets, plus deep-cleaned and sanitised car seats and strollers. from RM 40
  • Bike Spa, Coating & Helmet Tank-to-spokes spa with chain care, 1-year ceramic coating for the bike, and scratch removal plus coating for your helmet. from RM 40
