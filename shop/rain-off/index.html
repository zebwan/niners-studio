9NERS Studio Line

Rain Off

Siloxane glass coating — clear vision, better driving.

Rain Off puts a strong hydrophobic layer on your windscreen and glass: rain beads up and rolls away with the airflow, wipers work less, and night driving in a downpour stops being a guessing game. Spray, spread, buff — wipe and go.

  • End-user friendly — easy to use, perfect for first-timers
  • Strong water repellent — powerful beading for better visibility
  • Lasts up to 3 months — from a single application
  • Safe to use — on all automotive glass and windscreens

How to use

Five easy steps

Work panel by panel on clean, dry glass — out of direct sun.

1

Spray

Spray Rain Off onto the clean mirror or glass.

2

Spread

Spread it evenly across the surface.

3

Wait

Give it 1 minute to haze over.

4

Buff

Buff with a damp cloth.

5

Wipe

Wipe off any excess with a dry cloth — done.
