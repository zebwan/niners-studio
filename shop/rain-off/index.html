Rain Off

Hydrophobic glass coating spray for clear vision and better driving.

Rain Off puts a strong hydrophobic layer on your windscreen and glass: rain beads up and rolls away with the airflow, wipers work less, and night driving in a downpour stops being a guessing game. Spray, spread, buff, wipe and go.

  • End-user friendly, perfect for first-timers
  • Strong water repellent for better visibility in the rain
  • Lasts up to 3 months from a single application
  • Safe to use on all automotive glass and windscreens
  • Compact 100g can that's easy to keep in the car

How to use

Six easy steps

Work panel by panel on clean, dry glass, out of direct sun.

1

Prep

Start with clean, dry glass, free of watermarks.

2

Spray

Spray Rain Off onto the glass.

3

Spread

Spread it evenly with a clean microfiber cloth.

4

Wait

Give it about 1 minute to form a light haze.

5

Buff

Buff with a damp microfiber cloth.

6

Wipe dry

Finish with a dry cloth until the glass is crystal clear.

Not under direct sun

Don't apply in direct sunlight or on hot glass.

Shade & airflow

Work in a shaded, well-ventilated area.

Watermarks off first

Coating bonds best on glass that's free of watermarks.
